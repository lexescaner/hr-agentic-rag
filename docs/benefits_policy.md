# Benefits Policy

## Eligibility
Full-time employees become eligible for company-sponsored benefits on their first day of employment.
Part-time employees working 20+ hours per week become eligible after 90 days of continuous
employment. Contractors are not eligible for employer-sponsored benefits; see their individual
contract terms for any applicable arrangements.

## Health, Dental, and Vision Coverage
Eligible employees may enroll in one of two health plan tiers:
- **Standard PPO**: company covers 80% of premium for employee, 50% for dependents.
- **Premium PPO**: company covers 70% of premium for employee, 50% for dependents; broader
  provider network and lower out-of-pocket maximums.

Dental and vision coverage are optional add-ons available at employee cost, with a modest company
contribution reflected on each employee's annual benefits statement.

## Retirement Contributions
The company matches employee retirement contributions up to 6% of base salary, with matching
contributions vesting immediately (no vesting schedule).

## Enrollment Windows
- New hires must enroll within 30 days of their start date, or coverage begins on the following
  open enrollment cycle.
- Open enrollment occurs annually in November for coverage effective January 1.
- Qualifying life events (marriage, birth/adoption, loss of other coverage) allow enrollment outside
  the standard window; changes must be submitted within 30 days of the qualifying event.

## Employment Type and Benefits Eligibility
| Employment Type | Health/Dental/Vision | Retirement Match | PTO Accrual |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full-time | Eligible | Eligible | Eligible |
| Part-time (20+ hrs/wk, 90+ days) | Eligible | Eligible | Prorated |
| Contractor | Not eligible | Not eligible | Not eligible |

## Changes to Benefits Elections
Outside of open enrollment or a qualifying life event, benefits elections generally cannot be
changed mid-year. Employees with questions about their current elections should contact their HR
Business Partner.

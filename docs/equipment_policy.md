# Equipment Policy

## Standard Issue Equipment
All full-time and eligible part-time employees receive the following standard equipment at no cost:
- Company laptop (model determined by role: standard for most roles, high-performance for
  engineering/design roles)
- Charger and one set of standard peripherals (mouse, and keyboard for office-based roles)
- Headset for roles involving regular video calls

## Requesting Additional Equipment
Employees may request additional equipment (external monitor, ergonomic accessories, docking
station) through the IT request portal. Requests are typically fulfilled within 10 business days.
Home office equipment purchased independently may be eligible for reimbursement up to the annual cap
described in the Expense Reimbursement Policy.

## Equipment for Remote Work
Employees approved for remote work (see Remote Work Policy) may take standard-issue equipment to
their approved remote location. Equipment must remain within the security and data residency
constraints described in the Data Security Policy regardless of physical location.

## Replacement and Repair
- Hardware malfunctions should be reported to IT immediately via the internal ticketing system.
- Standard equipment is refreshed on a 3-year cycle, or sooner if repair costs exceed replacement
  value.
- Loss or damage due to employee negligence may result in the employee being responsible for
  replacement cost, subject to manager and HR review.

## Return of Equipment
All company-issued equipment must be returned within 5 business days of an employee's last working
day, using the return process outlined by IT during offboarding. Failure to return equipment may
result in the replacement cost being deducted from final pay where permitted by local law.

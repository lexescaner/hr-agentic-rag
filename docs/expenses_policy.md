# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Eligible Expenses
Employees may submit expenses for reimbursement in the following categories, provided they are
reasonable, business-related, and supported by a valid receipt:
- Business travel (flights, trains, ground transportation, lodging)
- Client meals and team meals during work travel
- Conference and training fees
- Home office equipment (see Equipment Policy for specifics)
- Mobile phone stipend for roles requiring after-hours availability

## Submission Requirements
- Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of being incurred.
- Receipts are required for any single expense over 25 EUR (or local currency equivalent).
- Expense reports must include a brief business justification for each line item.

## Approval Thresholds
- Expenses under 500 EUR: direct manager approval only.
- Expenses between 500 EUR and 2,000 EUR: direct manager plus department head approval.
- Expenses over 2,000 EUR: requires Finance pre-approval before the expense is incurred, not after.

## Travel Expense Specifics
- Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy may be approved for flights
  over 6 hours with manager sign-off.
- Lodging should not exceed the company's per-city rate guidance published internally by Finance.
- Personal expenses incurred during business travel (entertainment, mini-bar, personal calls) are not
  reimbursable.

## Home Office Equipment Expenses
One-time purchases such as a monitor, keyboard, or ergonomic chair may be reimbursed up to 500 EUR
per employee per calendar year, subject to manager approval. See the Equipment Policy for the full
list of pre-approved items and quantities.

## Non-Reimbursable Items
- Commuting costs between home and a regular office location
- Traffic fines, parking tickets, or toll violations
- Alcohol, except where explicitly part of an approved client entertainment budget

Public Holidays Policy

Observed Holidays

The company observes the standard public holidays of each employee's registered work location. A full list of observed holidays for each office is published annually by HR in December for the following calendar year. Remote employees observe the holiday calendar of their registered home country unless otherwise agreed with their manager.

Floating Holidays

In addition to standard public holidays, each employee receives 2 floating holidays per calendar year, which may be used for cultural, religious, or personal observances not included in the standard company holiday calendar. Floating holidays must be requested through the same process as PTO requests, with at least 5 business days' notice.

Holiday Pay for Non-Exempt Employees

Non-exempt employees required to work on an observed public holiday receive 1.5x their standard hourly rate for hours worked that day, in addition to standard holiday pay if eligible under local law.

Holidays Falling on a Weekend

When an observed holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the company designates the nearest weekday (typically the preceding Friday or following Monday) as the observed day off, per the published annual holiday calendar for that location.

Interaction with PTO

Observed public holidays do not count against an employee's PTO balance. See the PTO Policy for details on PTO accrual, requesting time off, and approval requirements. Floating holidays described above are also separate from standard PTO and do not draw down the PTO balance.

New Employees

Floating holidays are prorated in an employee's first calendar year based on start date, consistent with the proration approach described in the Onboarding Policy for other first-year benefits.

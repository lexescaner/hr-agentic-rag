# Onboarding Policy

## Before Day 1
- HR sends a welcome email with start date, reporting manager, and first-day logistics at least
  5 business days before the employee's start date.
- IT provisions accounts (email, SSO, core systems) so they are active by the start date.
- Equipment (laptop, peripherals) is shipped or made available for pickup ahead of Day 1 per the
  Equipment Policy.

## Week 1 Checklist
New employees should complete the following during their first week:
1. Complete mandatory compliance training (Code of Conduct, Data Security, Anti-Harassment).
2. Meet with their manager to review 30/60/90-day goals.
3. Set up direct deposit and confirm benefits enrollment window (see Benefits Policy).
4. Complete IT security onboarding (VPN setup, device encryption, MFA enrollment).
5. Schedule introductory meetings with immediate team members.

## 30-60-90 Day Milestones
- **Day 30**: manager check-in on early progress; benefits enrollment deadline (see Benefits Policy).
- **Day 60**: mid-point check-in; new hire should have completed all mandatory training.
- **Day 90**: formal onboarding review; part-time employees working 20+ hours/week become eligible
  for benefits at this milestone if not already enrolled (see Benefits Policy).

## Manager Responsibilities
Managers are responsible for assigning an onboarding buddy, scheduling the Week 1 check-ins listed
above, and confirming with HR that all compliance training is completed by Day 30.

## Remote and Hybrid Onboarding
Employees onboarding remotely follow the same checklist, with IT security onboarding conducted via
a scheduled video walkthrough rather than in-person setup. See the Remote Work Policy and Data
Security Policy for ongoing remote work requirements beyond the onboarding period.

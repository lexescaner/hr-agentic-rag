# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Eligibility
Full-time employees accrue PTO from their hire date. Standard annual allowance is 25 days per year,
increasing to 27 days after 3 years of continuous service, and 28 days after 5 years.
Contractors are not eligible for company PTO accrual; time off for contractors is governed by their
individual contract terms.

## Requesting PTO
Employees must submit PTO requests through the HR system at least 5 business days in advance for
requests of 3 days or fewer, and at least 15 business days in advance for requests of 4 or more
consecutive days.

## Manager Approval
All PTO requests require manager approval before being confirmed. Managers should respond to PTO
requests within 3 business days. If a manager does not respond within 5 business days, the request
escalates automatically to the HR Business Partner for the employee's region.

## Blackout Periods
Certain high-demand periods (e.g., the last two weeks of the fiscal year, major product launch weeks)
may be designated as blackout periods where PTO requests require additional approval from a director
or above. Blackout periods are communicated at least 30 days in advance.

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the following calendar year. Any unused days beyond
this cap are forfeited unless local law requires otherwise.

## Unlimited PTO Exception
This policy does not apply to employees on an unlimited PTO plan, which is only offered to certain
senior leadership roles as specified in their individual employment agreements.

# Remote Work Policy

## Standard Remote Work
Employees may work remotely from their registered home country up to 3 days per week without prior
approval, provided their role is designated as remote-eligible by their manager.

## Working From Another Country
Employees who wish to work from a country other than their registered home country for more than
10 consecutive business days must submit a Cross-Border Remote Work Request at least 20 business days
in advance. This is required regardless of the employee's citizenship or visa status.

Approval depends on:
- **Tax and legal risk**: whether working from the destination country creates a permanent
  establishment or payroll tax obligation for the company.
- **Data security**: whether the destination country meets the company's data residency and
  security requirements (see Data Security Policy).
- **Time zone overlap**: whether the employee can maintain at least 4 hours of overlap with their
  team's core working hours.

Requests exceeding 6 weeks in a single calendar year require additional Legal and Tax review and are
evaluated on a case-by-case basis; approval is not guaranteed.

## Equipment for Remote Work
Company-issued laptops and standard peripherals may be used at any approved remote work location.
Employees are responsible for maintaining a secure home network per the Data Security Policy.

## Approval Chain
Cross-Border Remote Work Requests require sign-off from: (1) the employee's direct manager,
(2) HR Business Partner, and (3) Legal/Tax, in that order. All three approvals must be recorded before
travel begins.

# Workplace Conduct Policy

## Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and professionalism.
This applies equally to in-person interactions, virtual meetings, and written communication (email,
chat, etc.), regardless of location or work arrangement.

## Anti-Harassment and Anti-Discrimination
The company prohibits harassment or discrimination based on race, gender, age, religion, disability,
sexual orientation, national origin, or any other legally protected characteristic. This includes:
- Verbal or written harassment
- Unwelcome physical contact
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith

Employees who experience or witness a violation should report it to their manager, HR Business
Partner, or through the confidential ethics hotline. Reports are investigated promptly, and
retaliation against a reporting employee is itself a violation of this policy.

## Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any outside employment, financial interest, or personal relationship that
could reasonably create a conflict of interest with their role. Disclosures are reviewed by HR and
handled confidentially.

## Confidentiality
Employees must not disclose confidential company information, client data, or trade secrets to
unauthorized parties, both during and after employment. See the Data Security Policy for technical
handling requirements of sensitive data.

## Escalation and Disciplinary Process
Violations of this policy are addressed through a progressive process: verbal warning, written
warning, performance improvement plan, and, for serious or repeated violations, termination.
Some violations (e.g. harassment, safety violations, data breaches) may warrant immediate escalation
to HR and, where applicable, immediate termination without progressing through earlier steps.

## Workplace Safety
Employees must report safety concerns immediately to their manager or facilities team. Remote
employees should refer to the Remote Work Policy and Data Security Policy for home-office-specific
safety and security expectations.
